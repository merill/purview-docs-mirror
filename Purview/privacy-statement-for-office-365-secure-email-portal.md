# Privacy statement for encrypted message portal | Microsoft Learn

Last Updated: January 2025

This privacy statement governs the encrypted message portal. Previous versions were known as Office 365 Message Encryption (OME), the "OME Portal", and the "New OME capabilities Portal". This privacy statement does not apply to other online or offline Microsoft sites, products, or services. Other privacy statements may also apply to the data you process through the new OME capabilities Portal, such as the privacy statement for Microsoft account (if it is used for authentication) or the privacy statement associated with your device.

## Collection, Processing, and Use of Your Information

The encrypted message portal enables you to view email encrypted with Microsoft 365 from various end points, such as desktop computers or mobile devices. The encrypted email arrives in your mailbox as an HTML attachment to a regular mail. If the mail is sent to your Microsoft account, work or school account, or Gmail account, you will be asked to sign in to the web-based encrypted message portal with such account. If the encrypted mail is sent to an account other than the Microsoft Account, the work or school account, or a Gmail account, you will be prompted to create a Microsoft account and associate it with the account to which the encrypted message was sent; alternatively, you can choose to request a one-time passcode for authentication, which will be sent to the same email address to which the encrypted message was sent. After successful authentication, the message will be decrypted and displayed via the encrypted message portal.

Your credential information to the Microsoft account, the work or school account, or the Gmail account, and the one-time passcode, will be used solely for authentication; it will not be stored in the encrypted message portal, or used by Microsoft 365 for any other purpose.

During the decryption process, the encrypted mail you receive will not be stored by the encrypted message portal; it will not be transmitted outside the encrypted message portal at any time.

The encrypted message portal may, in some instances, collect data points about the use and performance of Microsoft 365 Message Encryption (for example, how often encrypted messages are viewed, the browser or app used, and any errors encountered), which may be analyzed in aggregate for purposes of improving the quality, security, and integrity of this and other Microsoft apps, products, or services. This information is not used to identify, contact, or target advertising to you.
