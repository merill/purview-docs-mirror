# Learn about Microsoft Purview | Microsoft Learn

Microsoft Purview is a comprehensive set of solutions that helps your organization govern, protect, and manage data in the era of AI, wherever your data lives. Microsoft Purview solutions provide integrated coverage and help address the fragmentation of data across organizations, the lack of visibility that hampers data protection and governance, the rapid acceleration of AI transformation, and the blurring of traditional IT management roles.

Microsoft Purview combines solutions and services into a [unified platform](purview-portal) that helps your organization:

- Gain visibility into data across your organization.
- Safeguard and manage sensitive data across its lifecycle, wherever it lives.
- Govern data seamlessly in new, comprehensive ways.
- Manage critical data risks and regulatory requirements.
- Protect against accidental oversharing and sensitive data leakage in generative AI.

![Solution areas in Microsoft Purview.](media/purview-areas.png)

## Unified data security, governance, and compliance solutions for the era of AI

Microsoft Purview provides organizations with a powerful platform for seamlessly securing and governing data. This unified approach helps organizations reduce complexity and improve outcomes related to mitigating risks in the era of AI.

### Adopt AI with confidence

AI transformation is accelerating at an incredible pace, which compounds organizations' challenges around securing their data. It's never been more important for organizations to mitigate and manage the risks associated with AI usage, and implement corresponding protection and governance controls.

Microsoft Purview data security and compliance protections for generative AI apps helps you protect data however you use AI, whether it's:

- Copilot experiences and agents,
- Enterprise AI apps that you build, or
- Other AI apps you use.

Explore the full range of [Microsoft Purview capabilities for data security and compliance for AI](ai-microsoft-purview).

### Data security

Microsoft Purview provides a robust and coordinated set of [data security solutions](purview-security) to help you dynamically secure data throughout its lifecycle. The solutions include:

- [Microsoft Purview Data Loss Prevention](dlp-learn-about-dlp)
- [Microsoft Purview Data Security Investigations](data-security-investigations)
- [Microsoft Purview Information Barriers](information-barriers)
- [Microsoft Purview Information Protection](information-protection)
- [Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management](insider-risk-management-solution-overview)
- [Microsoft Purview Privileged Access Management](privileged-access-management-solution-overview)

Now in preview, [Microsoft Data Security Posture Management (DSPM)](data-security-posture-management-learn-about) helps you discover, protect, and investigate sensitive data risks across your digital estate. Unifying other Microsoft Purview solutions, DSPM provides visibility and control for both traditional applications and AI apps and agents, so you can monitor, assess, and remediate data risks regardless of where sensitive data resides.

### Data governance

Microsoft Purview includes unified [data governance solutions](data-governance-overview) for managing data services across your data estate and developing a federated approach to governing your data and unlocking business innovation. The solutions include:

- [Microsoft Purview Data Map](data-map)
- [Microsoft Purview Unified Catalog](unified-catalog)

### Data compliance

Microsoft Purview includes [data compliance solutions](purview-compliance) to help your organization minimize compliance risks and meet regulatory requirements. The solutions include:

- [Microsoft Purview Audit](audit-solutions-overview)
- [Microsoft Purview Communication Compliance](communication-compliance-solution-overview)
- [Microsoft Purview Compliance Manager](compliance-manager)
- [Microsoft Purview Data Lifecycle Management](data-lifecycle-management)
- [Microsoft Purview eDiscovery](edisc)
- [Microsoft Purview Records Management](records-management)

### Shared capabilities

Many capabilities that help you protect and govern data across Microsoft Purview are shared among multiple solutions. Some of these capabilities include:

- [Classifiers](data-classification-overview)
- [Connectors](archive-third-party-data)
- [Data explorer](data-classification-data-explorer) and [activity explorer](data-classification-activity-explorer)
- [Sensitivity labels](sensitivity-labels)
